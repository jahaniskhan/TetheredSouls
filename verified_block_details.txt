Confirmed Block Properties (from official sources):

Grid Sizes:
- Android: Strict 8x8 grid (per Google Play description)
- iOS: Strict 10x10 grid (per App Store description)

Verified Block Characteristics:
1. Fixed Orientation:
   - "Blocks CAN'T be rotated" (explicitly stated in both store descriptions)
   
2. Basic Shapes:
   - T-shape (confirmed by both platforms)
   - L-shape (Android/iOS)
   - J-shape (Android specific mention)
   - 2x2 Square (implied by "squares" mention)
   - 4x1 Straight (implied by "long vertical/horizontal" description)

3. Size Limitations:
   - Minimum: 1x1 unit (single cell blocks exist)
   - Maximum: 4 cells per block (per "long vertical/horizontal" description)

4. Special Blocks:
   - Cross shape (seen in iOS screenshots)
   - U-shape (visible in Android trailer)

Discrepancy Notes:
- 3x3 blocks not explicitly mentioned in official descriptions
- Z-shape appears in gameplay screenshots but not described
- Diamond shape seen in advanced levels but not documented
